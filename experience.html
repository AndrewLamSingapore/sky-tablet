THE SKY TABLET · EVIDENCE-AWARE WORLD

The interface is the sky.

The opening camera is composed as a historical tableau. Moving away from it reveals spatial geometry and clearly marked interpretive extensions. The experience preserves uncertainty instead of turning generated scenery into historical fact.

Evidence-basedMonumental mud-brick architecture, glazed-brick visual language, cuneiform culture, celestial observation and the Three Paths tradition.
InterpretiveExact architecture, placement, clothing, lighting, tablet display and cinematic choreography.
Imagined extensionUnobserved streets, courtyards and spatial continuity required for free exploration.
