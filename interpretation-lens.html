Three ways to interpret a reconstruction

Evidence

Physical artifacts, primary texts, and historically documented practices.

Interpretation

Scholarly inferences and modern playable rules that fill gaps in the surviving record.

Imagination

Creative presentation and educational interactions that do not claim ancient authenticity.
